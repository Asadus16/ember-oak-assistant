# Storage and Freshness

## Roast dates
Every bag has its roast date printed on the label. Coffee tastes best from about 4 to 14 days after roasting, and stays good for about 4 to 6 weeks. Because we roast to order, your coffee usually arrives within a week of its roast date.

## How to store coffee
- Keep beans in their original resealable bag or an airtight container, at room temperature, away from light, heat and moisture.
- Do not store beans in the refrigerator, because moisture and food smells get in.
- Freezing is fine for long storage: freeze in small airtight portions, and thaw a portion fully before opening it.

## Whole bean or ground
Whole bean stays fresh much longer. Grind just before brewing for the best flavor. Ground coffee loses aroma within days. If you do not own a grinder, the Manual Burr Grinder is our entry level option.

## Tasting set freshness
The Tasting Set bags are 3 oz each. They are packed with the roast date on each bag, and are best enjoyed within 3 to 4 weeks.
