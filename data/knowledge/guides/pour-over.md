# Pour-Over Guide (Ceramic Dripper)

## What you need
A size 02 cone dripper, size 02 paper filters, a gooseneck kettle, a scale, a carafe or mug, and freshly ground coffee. Light and medium roasts such as Ethiopia Guji Natural, Kenya AA Kiambu and Colombia Huila Washed suit this method best.

## Recipe for one large mug (about 300 ml)
- Coffee: 18 g, ground medium fine, like table salt.
- Water: 300 g at 93 to 96 degrees Celsius for light roasts, 90 to 93 degrees for medium and dark roasts.
- Total brew time: about 2 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes 30 seconds.

## Steps
1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water to remove paper taste and warm the dripper. Discard the rinse water.
2. Add the ground coffee and level the bed.
3. Bloom: pour 40 g of water over the grounds and wait 30 to 40 seconds.
4. Pour in slow circles up to 180 g by 1 minute 15 seconds, then to 300 g by about 2 minutes.
5. Let it draw down. Swirl gently, then serve.

## Troubleshooting
- Tastes sour or thin: grind finer or use hotter water.
- Tastes bitter or dry: grind coarser or use slightly cooler water.
- Drawdown takes too long: grind coarser.
- Drawdown is very quick and the cup is weak: grind finer.
