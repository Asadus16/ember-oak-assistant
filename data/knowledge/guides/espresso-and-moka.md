# Espresso and Moka Pot Guide

## Espresso starting recipe
Oak Espresso Blend and Brazil Cerrado are roasted for espresso. This is a starting point for a standard machine with a double basket:
- Dose: 18 g of coffee, ground fine.
- Yield: 36 g of espresso in the cup (a 1 to 2 ratio).
- Time: 25 to 30 seconds from the first drip.
- Temperature: 92 to 94 degrees Celsius.

## Dialing in
Change one thing at a time and taste.
- Shot runs fast (under 22 seconds) and tastes sour: grind finer.
- Shot runs slow (over 35 seconds) and tastes bitter or harsh: grind coarser.
- Weak and watery: raise the dose slightly or grind finer.
Grinders vary, so use the roast date and your own taste, not a fixed setting. The Electric Burr Grinder has 40 settings, which makes small changes easy. The Manual Burr Grinder can reach espresso, but its stepped adjustment makes fine tuning harder.

## Moka pot
The Stainless Moka Pot 6-cup makes about 300 ml of strong coffee, not true espresso.
1. Fill the base with hot water up to the valve, not over it.
2. Fill the basket with medium fine coffee, level, without tamping.
3. Heat on medium. Remove the pot from the heat when it starts to sputter and run the base under cold water to stop it.
Oak Espresso Blend, Brazil Cerrado and Sumatra Mandheling work well.
