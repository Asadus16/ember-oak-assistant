# Cold Brew Guide

## Recipe (concentrate)
Cold Brew Blend is roasted for this method, and can be bought ground coarse at checkout.
- Coffee: 100 g, ground coarse.
- Water: 800 g of cold, filtered water.
- Steep: 12 to 16 hours in the refrigerator or at cool room temperature.

## Steps
1. Combine coffee and water in a jar or French press and stir so all grounds are wet.
2. Cover and steep 12 to 16 hours.
3. Strain through a paper filter or the French press plunger, then strain again through a paper filter for a cleaner cup.
4. Store the concentrate in the refrigerator.

## Serving
Dilute the concentrate 1 to 1 with cold water or milk over ice, then adjust to taste. Concentrate keeps up to 10 days refrigerated, but tastes best in the first week.

## Tip
For a quicker version, use the Glass Carafe Server as the jar, and strain with the Reusable Steel Filter followed by a paper filter.
