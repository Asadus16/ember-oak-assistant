# Choosing a Coffee

## Roast levels
- Light roast: brighter, fruity or floral, higher perceived acidity. Ethiopia Guji Natural and Kenya AA Kiambu.
- Medium roast: balanced, sweet, caramel and chocolate. Colombia Huila Washed, Guatemala Antigua, Ember House Blend, Swiss Water Decaf Colombia and Cold Brew Blend.
- Medium to dark and dark roast: heavier body, chocolate and nut, lower acidity. Brazil Cerrado, Oak Espresso Blend and Sumatra Mandheling.

## Which coffee for what you like
- Fruity and bright: Ethiopia Guji Natural or Kenya AA Kiambu, brewed as pour-over.
- Chocolate and caramel, smooth: Colombia Huila Washed, Guatemala Antigua or Ember House Blend.
- Strong, low acidity, milk drinks: Brazil Cerrado or Oak Espresso Blend.
- Caffeine free: Swiss Water Decaf Colombia.
- Iced coffee: Cold Brew Blend.

## Which coffee for each brew method
- Pour-over and AeroPress: light and medium roasts.
- French press and drip machines: medium and dark roasts, such as Ember House Blend.
- Espresso and moka pot: Oak Espresso Blend or Brazil Cerrado.

## Not sure
The Tasting Set has four coffees across the range and is a good way to find your favorite.

## Grind size by method
Espresso: fine. Moka pot: medium fine. Pour-over and drip: medium to medium fine. French press and cold brew: coarse.
