# French Press Guide

## What you need
A French press (the Glass French Press 34 oz makes up to four mugs), a scale, a kettle and coarse ground coffee. Medium and dark roasts such as Ember House Blend, Sumatra Mandheling and Brazil Cerrado give a rich cup.

## Recipe for the 34 oz press (about 1 litre)
- Coffee: 60 g, ground coarse, like sea salt.
- Water: 1000 g at 93 to 96 degrees Celsius.
- Steep time: 4 minutes.

## Steps
1. Add the coffee to the empty, warmed press.
2. Pour all the water, wetting all the grounds, and start a timer.
3. Place the lid on with the plunger up, without pressing.
4. At 4 minutes, stir gently once, then press slowly.
5. Pour all the coffee out right away. Coffee left on the grounds keeps extracting and turns bitter.

## Tips
- A fine grind makes the cup muddy and hard to press. Grind coarser.
- For a lighter cup, steep 3.5 minutes. For a stronger cup, use a little more coffee, not a longer steep.
- The Travel Press Mug works the same way at a smaller scale: use about 20 g of coffee for 12 oz.
