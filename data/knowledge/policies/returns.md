# Returns and Refunds

## Gear
Unused gear in its original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. You pay the return shipping unless the item arrived damaged or defective. Contact support to get a return authorization before you send anything back.

## Coffee
Because coffee is roasted to order, we cannot accept returns of coffee once it has shipped. If your coffee arrives damaged, or we sent the wrong item, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

If you simply do not enjoy a coffee, tell us. We cannot refund it, but we will suggest a different roast, and often send a small replacement bag.

## Warranty
Defective gear is covered by a 1 year warranty. Grinders have a 2 year warranty. The warranty does not cover damage from drops, misuse or normal wear of burrs and seals.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after we receive the return or approve the claim. Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the return is our error.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled for a full refund before it has been roasted or packed. See the Orders and Account policy for the time window.
