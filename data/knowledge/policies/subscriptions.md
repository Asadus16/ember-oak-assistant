# Subscriptions

## What you get
A subscription sends one 12 oz bag of the coffee you choose on a schedule of every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. The subscription price is 10 percent below the single bag price and standard shipping is always free.

## Managing your subscription
You can skip a shipment, pause the subscription, change the coffee, change the frequency or cancel at any time. Changes must be made at least 48 hours before your next roast date. A change made later applies from the following shipment.

## Payments
Your card is charged when the coffee is roasted. If a payment fails we retry after 2 days and again after 5 days. If it still fails, the subscription is paused and we email you to update your card.

## Cancelling
Cancelling stops all future shipments. A shipment that has already been roasted cannot be cancelled and follows the normal Returns policy.

## Gifts
Subscriptions are for one recipient address. A gift subscription is not available. For a gift, choose the Tasting Set instead.
