# Shipping

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally at this time. We cannot ship to PO boxes with UPS, but USPS delivery to a PO box is available at checkout.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of 50 dollars or more. Orders under 50 dollars pay a flat 6.95 dollars for standard shipping. Orders to Alaska and Hawaii add 8 dollars. Subscription orders always ship free.

Express shipping is 14.95 dollars and arrives in 2 business days after the order ships.

## How fast orders leave
Coffee is roasted to order on Monday to Thursday. Orders placed before 12:00 noon Pacific time on a weekday are roasted that day. Orders placed after noon, or on Friday to Sunday, are roasted the next roasting day. Gear ships within 1 business day.

Coffee usually ships 1 to 2 business days after the order is placed. Standard shipping then takes 3 to 5 business days.

## Tracking
You get an email with a tracking number as soon as your order ships. You can also ask this assistant for your order status with your order number and the email used at checkout.

## Delays
Weather and carrier problems can delay delivery. If your tracking has not moved for 5 business days, contact support and we will open a claim with the carrier.
