# Orders and Account

## Changing or cancelling an order
An order can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed, or until it has been roasted or packed, whichever comes first. After that it cannot be changed. Contact support as quickly as you can, with your order number.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only before the order ships. Contact support with your order number and the new address. We cannot change the address once tracking exists.

## Checking order status
The store assistant can look up an order when you give it the order number and the email address used at checkout. It shows the status, the carrier and tracking number, the estimated delivery date and the items. It never shows names, full addresses or payment details, and it cannot change an order.

Order statuses:
- Processing: the order is received and waiting to be roasted or packed.
- Roasting: your coffee is being roasted and packed.
- Shipped: the order has left us and has a tracking number.
- Delivered: the carrier has delivered the order.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled before shipping.
- Refunded: the order was refunded.

## Payment safety
Never send card numbers in chat. The assistant cannot take payments, and we will never ask for a full card number in chat, email or on WhatsApp.
