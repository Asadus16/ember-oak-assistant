# Support, Gifts and Wholesale

## Contacting support
Email support@emberandoak.example. Support answers Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Pacific time, and replies within 1 business day. The store assistant can answer questions about products, brewing, shipping and orders, but it cannot issue refunds, change orders or take payments. Those need a human on the support team.

## Gifts
We do not sell gift cards. The Tasting Set (four 3 oz bags in a gift box with a brewing card) is our gift option. A gift note can be added at checkout. We do not ship gifts to more than one address in a single order.

## Wholesale
We supply cafes and offices. The minimum wholesale order is 10 lb per month. For pricing and a sample kit, email wholesale@emberandoak.example. The store assistant does not quote wholesale prices.

## Allergens and facility
Our coffee is roasted in a facility that does not process nuts. Flavor notes such as almond or hazelnut describe taste only and do not mean the coffee contains nuts.
