# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you sell decaf?
Yes. Swiss Water Decaf Colombia is a medium roast decaf made with the Swiss Water Process.

## Can I buy ground coffee?
Yes, coffee can be ground at checkout for your brew method. Whole bean stays fresh longer, so it is our recommendation.

## How big are the bags?
Coffee bags are 12 oz (340 g). The Tasting Set has four 3 oz (85 g) bags.

## Do you sell espresso machines?
No. We sell coffee, grinders, brewers and accessories only. We do not sell espresso machines.

## Do you sell gift cards?
No. The Tasting Set is our gift option.

## Do you ship internationally?
No. We only ship within the United States.

## What is the free shipping minimum?
Standard shipping is free on orders of 50 dollars or more, and always on subscriptions.

## Can I change my order after I placed it?
Only within 2 hours of placing it, or until it has been roasted or packed. See the Orders and Account policy.

## What is your return policy?
Unused gear can be returned within 30 days. Coffee cannot be returned once shipped, but damaged or wrong items are replaced or refunded if reported within 7 days.

## Is the Sumatra Mandheling available?
Availability changes. Ask the assistant to check the live stock, or look at the product page.

## Are your grinders good for espresso?
The Electric Burr Grinder reaches espresso and has 40 settings. The Manual Burr Grinder can reach espresso, but its stepped adjustment makes fine tuning harder.

## Is the Gooseneck Kettle induction compatible?
No. The Gooseneck Kettle works on gas and electric hobs, and the base is not induction compatible.

## How do I contact a person?
Email support@emberandoak.example. Support replies within 1 business day, Monday to Friday.
